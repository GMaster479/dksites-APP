Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026 · DK Sites LLC, Connecticut, USA

The short version

  • We collect what it takes to build, host, and bill for your website: your email, your business details, the files you upload, and your purchase records.
  • We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use ad trackers in the Builder.
  • We share data only with the companies that help run the service (for example, Stripe for payments and Cloudflare for hosting), and only to run the service.
  • You can ask us to see, correct, or delete your information anytime: dylan@dksitesllc.com.

This policy covers DK Sites Builder at app.dksites.com, the preview sites at *.dksites.com, and the websites we host for customers (together, the “Service”). The Service is run by DK Sites LLC (“DK Sites,” “we,” “us”). If you have a question about this policy, email dylan@dksitesllc.com.

1. What we collect

Account information

When you create an account or sign in, we collect your email address. If you choose “Continue with Google,” we also receive your name and your Google account ID from Google. We record when you created your account and when you last signed in. Signing in by email uses one-time links that expire after 15 minutes. We don’t use passwords.

Business information

To build your site, we use the details you give us (your business name, city, and a description) and publicly available information about the business, such as its Google Business Profile listing (name, address, phone, hours, rating, reviews, and photos), its Yelp listing, its current website, and public domain-registration (WHOIS) records for domains you enter.

Files and content you add

This includes the logos, menus, photos, and other files you upload, images you ask us to import from a web address, and the edit instructions you type into the editor.

Purchase and billing information

This includes the domain you buy or connect, what you paid and when, your renewal date, and whether you turned on auto-renew. Payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number. We store the reference IDs Stripe gives us (a customer ID, and a saved-payment-method ID if you turn on auto-renew) so we can show your receipts and renew your site if you asked us to.

Technical information

Our servers receive your IP address and basic request details. We use them to keep the Service running, to limit abuse (for example, capping how many sites one address can generate), and to fix problems.

Cookies and browser storage

We set one cookie, dks_session, to keep you signed in. It’s required for your account to work, and it lasts up to 30 days or until you sign out. The Builder also saves your in-progress build in your browser’s local storage so a refresh doesn’t lose your work. If you use Google sign-in, Google’s sign-in button loads from Google and is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy. We don’t use advertising cookies or analytics trackers in the Builder.

2. How we use it

  • To generate, edit, preview, publish, and host your website.
  • To sign you in and keep your previews and sites tied to your account.
  • To process payments, register or connect your domain, and handle renewals, including auto-renew charges only if you turned that on.
  • To send service emails: sign-in links, launch updates, and renewal reminders 30 and 7 days before your renewal date. Renewal emails may also tell you about a custom DK Sites design service. Reply or email us if you’d like those to stop.
  • To answer your questions, prevent fraud and abuse, keep the Service secure, and meet legal, tax, and accounting obligations.

We don’t sell your personal information, we don’t share it for advertising, and we don’t use it to train AI models.

3. Information from Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we receive only your name, email address, and Google account ID. We use them only to create your account and sign you in. We don’t ask for access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google data. We don’t use Google user data for advertising, we don’t sell it, and we don’t transfer it to anyone except as needed to run the Service or as required by law. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5. What’s public

Websites are public by nature. Preview sites (for example, yourbusiness.dksites.com) can be opened by anyone who has the link, though we tell search engines not to index them. Live sites on your domain are fully public. Don’t put anything on your site that you don’t want the public to see.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account and site records: while you have an account, and afterward for as long as needed for legal, tax, and accounting purposes (purchase records generally up to 7 years).
  • Previews that were never purchased: we may delete them after a period of inactivity.
  • Backups: nightly backups are kept for 30 days and then deleted automatically.
  • Sign-in links: they expire after 15 minutes and work only once.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See, correct, or delete your information: email dylan@dksitesllc.com from your account’s email address. We’ll respond within 30 days. Deleting your account takes down your previews. If you have a paid live site, we’ll work out with you what happens to the site and the domain before we remove anything.
  • Auto-renew: turn it on or off anytime from your dashboard.
  • Emails: sign-in and renewal emails are part of the service. Ask us to stop the custom-design offers anytime.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws. These can include the right to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of your data, and to appeal a decision we make about your request. Email us and we’ll honor them.

8. Security

Every connection uses HTTPS. Sign-in links and session tokens are stored only in scrambled (hashed) form. Payment details stay with Stripe. Access to our systems is limited to DK Sites. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we’ll notify you as the law requires.

9. Where your data is stored

DK Sites is based in the United States. Our application server is in Finland (EU), and our providers may process data in the US and other countries. Using the Service means your information may be handled in those places, under the protections described here.

10. Children

The Service is for businesses and adults. It isn’t meant for anyone under 18, and we don’t knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll also email account holders before it takes effect.
